BestRate is a mobile app concept for comparing currency exchange and remittance providers in one place.

The interface brings together the currency pair, sending amount, provider quotes and fees. Rather than stopping at the headline rate, it makes room for the amount a recipient would receive.

The design uses a navy background, teal highlights and a consistent card layout. The demonstration extends the original screens into a connected flow, including the content below the first screen.
