Codu

Android app · Gamified coding education

Learn to code.
Play to progress.

A beginner-friendly coding app that turns practice into levels, rewards and quiz duels. Learn Python, C++, JavaScript or Java, then put your skills to the test against a friend.

Codu in motion

Start with a language

Pick Python, C++, JavaScript or Java and carry on where you left off.

    01 · Why Codu

    Coding is hard.
    Games help.

    Learning to code can feel intimidating: unfamiliar words, no clear next step, and little sense of progress. Gamified learning has become popular because levels, streaks and rewards turn practice into something you want to come back to. Codu brings that to programming, and adds real-time duels so you can test what you've learned against someone else.

    • 01

      Gamified learning

      Levels, hearts, stars and streaks make every lesson feel like a game, not homework.

    • 02

      Duel mode

      Compete in real-time programming quizzes, with points based on speed and accuracy.

    • 03

      Beginner friendly

      Simple lessons in Python, C++, JavaScript and Java, with difficulty that rises fairly.

    • 04

      Better with friends

      Add friends, challenge them and compare trophies on the leaderboard.

    02 · Getting started

    From sign-up
    to first lesson.

    Join in a few taps

    Register with an email address, or use a Google or Facebook account. A 6-digit code verifies the email, then you choose the character that represents you. Forgot your password? The app emails a link to reset it.

    03 · The mascot

    A friendly face
    at every step.

    Keeps it warm, not intimidating

    A little robot makes Codu feel welcoming for beginners. It says welcome back when you log in, reassures you when you forget your password, offers help on the home screen, and turns a lost level into a playful 404 instead of a harsh failure.

    04 · Learning

    Small lessons.
    Visible progress.

    Follow a path, one level at a time

    Pick a language on the home screen, then work along a path that shows your progress and the stars you've earned. An assessment every 5 levels asks you to write code from scratch.

    Practise with hearts on the line

    Questions are short and interactive. A wrong answer costs a heart and shows what went wrong, and running out of hearts ends the attempt, so every answer counts.

    Stars, streaks and badges

    Finish a level to earn up to 3 stars, based on the hearts you have left. Completing a level each day builds your streak, and hidden missions unlock badges to collect on your profile.

    05 · Duel mode

    Learn together.
    Play to win.

    Choose a language and Codu finds an opponent with a similar number of trophies, or challenge a friend directly. Both players answer programming questions, and speed and accuracy decide the points.

    06 · Friends and support

    Better together.
    Help on hand.

    Add friends, then challenge them

    Your profile shows your streak, trophies and badges. Open your Friend List to see friends and challenge them to a duel, or tap Add to find someone new and send a request.

    Climb the leaderboard

    Winning duels earns trophies. Global and Friends leaderboards show who's on top, whether that's the whole app or just your group.

    A chatbot for quick help

    In the Help Center, type a question or pick one from the FAQ list. The chatbot answers, so you can get unstuck and carry on.
