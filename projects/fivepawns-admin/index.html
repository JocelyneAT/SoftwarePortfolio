FivePawns admin

Admin dashboard · Laptop and phone

Every booking.
One dashboard.

The staff side of FivePawns. Every booking made on the website lands here, so the team can run the day from a laptop or a phone.

  • ProjectIndividual
  • RoleDesign and development
  • ToolsReact · Firebase
  • Used byStaff and owner

The admin dashboard in motion

The day at a glance

Bookings, hours in use, money paid and money still owed, for any day.

    The real dashboard is private, so these screens use sample data. It's in Indonesian, for the team who use it.

    01 · Why it matters

    From notes by hand
    to one live view.

    • For staff

      BeforeRecord every booking by hand.

      NowEach booking from the website appears in the schedule by itself.

    • For the owner

      BeforeTrack bookings and payments by hand.

      NowSee what's booked and paid in one place, and update prices and hours there too.

    02 · The schedule

    Every table,
    every hour.

    Each table gets a row and each hour a column, so gaps and clashes are easy to spot. Colours show which bookings are paid, due at the counter, waiting for a check or finished.

    • Today's numbersBookings, hours in use, money paid and money still owed sit at the top.
    • Who's playing nowA live strip shows which tables are in use and which are free.
    • Take it awayOpen any booking for its details, or download the day's list as a CSV.

    03 · Live tables

    Know when
    time is up.

    Every table has a countdown to the end of its booking, so staff know when to stop a game without checking the list.

    • BlueThe game is running and there's plenty of time left.
    • OrangeTen minutes left, with the next booking shown beside it.
    • RedTime's up, and the timer counts the overtime.

    04 · Payments

    Check the proof.
    Confirm the table.

    From proof to paid in two taps

    When a customer uploads a QRIS payment, it waits here. Staff open the proof, compare the amount with the payment received, then approve or reject it.

    • Reject with a reasonA rejected payment goes back to the customer with a note explaining why.
    • Tables don't stay stuckBookings with no proof after 30 minutes cancel themselves, so the table is free again.
    • Everything updatesOnce approved, the booking shows as paid in the schedule.

    05 · Feedback and settings

    Hear customers.
    Run the business.

    • Feedback in one placeMessages from the website, with an average rating and a filter by stars.
    • Owner settingsVenue details, opening hours, tables, prices, promos, QRIS and the words on the website.
    • Two levels of accessOnly owners see settings. Staff get what they need for the day, and access can be removed at any time.

    Now see the customer side.
