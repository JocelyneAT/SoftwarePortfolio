FivePawns

Website and mobile · Semarang, Indonesia

Book a table.
Bring your people.

A real project of mine for FivePawns, a mahjong and board-game venue in Semarang. One website that works on a laptop and a phone: book a table, count your points and tell the venue how it went.

FivePawns in motion

One site, website and mobile

How to book, the location and today's promo are all on the homepage.

    02 · Booking

    From a free table
    to a confirmed one.

    One decision at a time

    Pick the date and hours, pick the table, then pay. Your choices carry forward, and the total stays at the bottom of the screen, right next to the button.

    • See what's freeAvailable, selected and full slots each look different, so nobody books a taken table.
    • Pay the local wayScan QRIS and upload proof, or pay at the venue. Prices are in rupiah.
    • Finish on WhatsAppOne tap opens a chat with the booking details already written. The chat shown here is a mock-up.

    03 · Count your points

    Your hand.
    Your score.

    No more counting by hand

    Counting mahjong points slows a game down. Players tap the tiles they hold and the calculator does the rest, then shows where every point came from.

    • Tap, don't typePlayers choose from pictures of the real tiles. Tap one in your hand to take it back.
    • Explain the resultThe breakdown lists each scoring item, so the total never feels like a mystery.
    • Show the rulesHong Kong rules with a 3 faan minimum and a 10 faan limit, stated at the top.

    04 · Feedback

    A direct line
    to the owner.

    Happy or not, one tap away

    A feedback button sits in the menu on every page. Choose a star rating if you like, write a suggestion or complaint, and send. It opens over the page, so nobody loses their place.

    • Straight to the dashboardEach message lands in the owner's own feedback tab, ready to read.
